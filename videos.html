Identifying AI-Generated Videos

Analysing Video Details

All of the indications that an image is AI-generated also applies to videos. Things like unidentifiable small details, things that don't make sense, and things looking overly perfect. These giveaways are even more apparent in AI-generated videos, and AI-generated videos tend to look a lot less realistic than images.

Video Length

One of the biggest giveaways for an AI-generated video is its length. Generating videos with AI is expensive, AI tools often limit the length of a video to be around 10 seconds. If one were to get around this by stitching multiple AI-generated video clips together, the result would be a video with cuts every 10 or so seconds.

Quality

Due to small details being a massive giveaway for AI-generated content, creators of AI-generated videos will often intentionally lower the quality of their video in order to obscure those fine details which might be giveaways.
