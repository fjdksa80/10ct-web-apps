Identifying AI-Generated Audio

SynthID and Content Credentials

Like images, audio files can be checked for signs of being AI-generated through SynthID and Content Credentials. To check for SynthID, ask Google Gemini if an audio file has SynthID watermarks. You can also verify OpenAI-generated audio with this tool.

Signs of AI-Synthesised Voice

Flat Emotion

Humans naturally change the pitch and volume of our speech over a sentence. We emphasise certain words to create meaning. AI-generated voices often sound static with constant volume and pitch. If they do change pitch, it often sounds unnatural, as if it's changing in the wrong spots. AI-generated voices don't place emphasis in the right spots, sometimes they don't have emphasis at all.

Lack of Pauses and Filler Words

When humans speak, we often pause or use filler words like "um" or "uh" while we think. AI-generated voices will not have these, making it sound as though they are reading a script. While a person making the AI-generated voice could manually add in pauses, when the AI reads it, it will sound unnatural and forced.

Lack of Sound Blends

In human speech, we often blend sounds together in the natural flow of speech. Phrases like "don't you" or "what are you" become "dontcha" and "whatcha". AI-generated voices rarely do this. Instead, it will sound every word out separately.

Same Delivery of Repeated Phrases

When an AI-generated voice repeats a phrase, it will often use the same intonation and inflection both times, whereas a human would change it up, using different emphasis or tone.

Mispronouncing Poper Nouns

AI-generated voices will often not know how to pronounce a word that isn't a common dictionary word. Things like proper nouns or even just more uncommon or specific words, AIs will not know how to pronounce and will take a guess based on how it is spelled.
